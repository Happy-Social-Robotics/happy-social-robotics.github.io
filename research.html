Research

Research Directions

Three directions that connect perception, learning, and real-world use of social robots.

Emotion-Aware Interaction

We develop robot behaviors that perceive social cues and respond naturally in classrooms, homes, and public spaces.

Embodied Learning

Our systems learn from multimodal data to support adaptive, personalized interaction in real-world environments.

Deployment for Impact

We build robust prototypes that can be evaluated with users and translated into practical social applications.
