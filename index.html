Student Robotics Group

Designing social robots that make people happy

Inspired by leading robotics labs, we combine AI, interaction design, and embodied systems to create practical, human-centered robotics at the University of Tsukuba.

Explore our work

Focus Areas

  • Human-Robot Interaction
  • Assistive Social Robotics
  • Interactive AI Systems

Research Directions

Emotion-Aware Interaction

We develop robot behaviors that perceive social cues and respond naturally in classrooms, homes, and public spaces.

Embodied Learning

Our systems learn from multimodal data to support adaptive, personalized interaction in real-world environments.

Deployment for Impact

We build robust prototypes that can be evaluated with users and translated into practical social applications.

Highlighted Projects

Companion Robot for Campus Wellbeing

A conversational platform designed to improve daily motivation and emotional support for students.

Interactive Learning Assistant

A robot assistant that supports collaborative learning through adaptive prompts and shared task guidance.

About Our Group

We are a small, interdisciplinary student team from the University of Tsukuba, collaborating across robotics, AI, and design to build socially meaningful systems.
