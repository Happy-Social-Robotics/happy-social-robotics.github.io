The pioneers

CB

Cynthia Breazeal

MIT Media Lab · Kismet, then Jibo

In the late 1990s, robots handled objects. Breazeal wanted one that handled people. Her robot Kismet had big eyes, moving ears and a voice, and people treated it like a baby. Her 2000 MIT thesis described it. She later founded the company behind Jibo, a home robot.
