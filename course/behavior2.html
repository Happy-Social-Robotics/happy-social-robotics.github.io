How robots signal

Voice and timing

A pause, a quick reply or an interruption each signals something.

VaryPitch, pause length, response latencyMeasureEngagement, perceived intelligence
Evidence and open question

Evidence. Kismet did not use language. It babbled, and its turn-taking felt like conversation, per MIT AI Lab clip descriptions. In studies, speech is often controlled by a hidden operator (Unit 7).

Open question. How much does response timing change trust and engagement?

MIT Kismet pages

Space and motion

A robot that moves among people is already behaving socially, with or without a face.

VaryPath, speed, passing distanceMeasureComfort, distance kept, avoidance
Evidence and open question

Evidence. Stanford’s Jackrabbot team turned campus video into coordinates and trained Social LSTM to learn human-human interaction from data, reproducing collision avoidance and group movement. Your “participants” are often a dataset: part vision, part social science (CVPR, ICRA and HRI).

Open question. How do people read a robot’s intent from its path, and how should it signal that it will yield?

Alahi et al. 2016

Adaptation

Behavior that changes with the person over time.

VaryPersonalized versus fixed behaviorMeasureEngagement over sessions
Evidence and open question

Evidence. Tega updates a belief about a child’s engagement as a story goes on. The 2026 LEAP-HRI workshop takes this to lifelong learning. Most studies are still single sessions, so multi-week studies stand out.

Open question. What does adaptation do to trust and care over weeks?
