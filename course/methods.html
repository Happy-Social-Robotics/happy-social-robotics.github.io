Methods and ethics

A reviewer checks your question, design, measures and treatment of participants, in that order.

The story

A robot tutor is built, a study is run, and the results look great. Then a reviewer asks one question: who was secretly driving the robot? In a review of 54 Wizard of Oz experiments, only about 5% reported training the wizard.

1. From topic to question

An illustrative example, not from a paper.

FieldSocial robots in education
Population, settingPreschoolers hearing a story at school
BehaviorRobot gaze: toward the book or the child
OutcomeHow much of the story the child recalls
QuestionDoes gaze toward the book increase recall?

A good question has an independent variable, a dependent variable and a comparison. Units 5 and 6 say why you expect a difference.
