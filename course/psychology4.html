The psychology of meeting a robot

4. Mind perception

ClaimPeople judge “mind” on two separate dimensions: Experience and Agency.
Evidence

Participants compared the mental capacities of human and nonhuman characters. Factor analysis gave Experience (feeling) and Agency (self-control). They predicted different moral judgments.

Gray et al. 2007
Implication

“Intelligent” and “has feelings” measure different things. Ask the one you mean.

Open question

Which design choices move agency and experience separately, and what does that do to blame, care and trust?
