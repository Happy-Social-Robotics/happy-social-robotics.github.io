Publishing your first paper

You do not need a full conference paper. Short, early-stage venues exist on purpose.

The story

You do not need a finished study to get on a conference program. HRI’s Late Breaking Reports are described as a way for researchers new to the field to take part: two to four pages, a poster and a first audience.

Dates checked 29 Oct 2026, on official conference sitesDeadlines are usually 23:59 Anywhere on Earth (UTC−12), the last moment of the day anywhere. Confirm before you plan.

DeadlineConference

No data yet?Do not rush 8 December. A well-argued position paper with a clear question and a study plan is an accepted workshop format and builds toward a real study.

From idea to poster

  1. 1QuestionOne population, one setting, one behavior (Unit 7)
  2. 2Co-authorA supervisor; ethics review
  3. 3PilotSmall study, video study, design probe
  4. 4Write2–4 pages in the venue’s template (ACM sigconf)
  5. 5SubmitSome venues are double-blind
  6. 6Camera-readyNames, affiliations, rights
  7. 7PresentUsually a poster, often in person
  8. 8ExtendGrow it into a full paper if non-archival
