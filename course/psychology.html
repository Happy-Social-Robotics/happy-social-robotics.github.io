The psychology of meeting a robot

Six ideas explain most of what you will read in HRI papers.

The story

In experiments, people who knew they were using a machine still showed it politeness. Nobody asked them to. This unit is about why, and what it means for anyone who builds a robot.

1. People respond socially to machines

“Thanks!”→
ClaimPeople apply everyday social rules to machines without deciding to, and without believing the machine is human.
Evidence

Experiments show gender stereotypes, politeness and reciprocity toward computers. Deliberate anthropomorphism did not explain it, so the authors call it mindless.

Reeves & Nass 1996Nass & Moon 2000
Implication

A robot need not be convincing to be treated socially. Every cue you add or remove is an experimental decision.

Open question

Most work used screens. What holds when the machine shares the room, moves and touches?
