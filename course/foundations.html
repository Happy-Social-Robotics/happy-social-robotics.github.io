What makes a robot social?

Appearance is not the test. Interaction is.

ClaimA robot is social when social interaction with people is important to what it is for.
Evidence and open question
Evidence

Fong, Nourbakhsh and Dautenhahn review “socially interactive robots” and build a taxonomy around that idea.

Fong et al. 2003
Implication

Judge a design by the interaction it must support. Neither a robot that yields in a corridor nor one that comforts a patient needs a human look.

Open question

Where is the boundary? Voice assistants, telepresence robots and named robot vacuums all sit near the line.
