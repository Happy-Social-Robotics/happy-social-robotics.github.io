The story

Prague, 1920. A writer named Karel Čapek is finishing a play about artificial workers who rebel. He cannot decide what to call them. His brother Josef, a painter, suggests robota, the Czech word for forced labor. When R.U.R. opened in 1921, the world got a new word.
