The story

In 2016 a knee-high ball on wheels rolled down a Stanford hallway. It had no face and never spoke, yet it had to know when to step aside for you. Two short videos show how a robot can be social without saying a word.

1. Meet Jackrabbot: The social robot

A knee-high, ball-shaped robot learns how people share sidewalks and hallways.

2. The Rise of Personal Robots, Cynthia Breazeal

The founder of MIT's Personal Robots Group explains why she built robots that interact with people instead of with objects.
