Publishing your first paper

What recent HRI workshop papers looked like

HRI 2027 workshop pages do not exist yet. These 2026 workshops (Edinburgh, 16 March) show the range.

LEAP-HRI

Lifelong learning and personalization in long-term HRI

  • Posted on site
  • Not anonymized
  • 7-minute talks
  • Final deadline 16 Feb

D-TUR

Designing transparent and understandable robots

  • Up to 6 pages
  • Double-blind, OpenReview
  • Deadline 20 Feb

Failing Forward

Design and deployment lessons from real-world HRI

  • 1–4 pages, LBR format
  • Plus a poster

Errors, Mistakes, and Failures

Errors in humans and robots, trust, recovery

  • ACM format
  • Deadline 27 Feb
  • Early-career welcome

InterAI

Interactive AI for human-centered robotics

  • 2–4 pages
  • Work in progress OK
  • arXiv papers OK
  • Deadline 20 Feb

The pattern: a short paper, 2–6 pages, on a theme narrow enough for a beginner to contribute one case or lesson. Many do not anonymize, and some do not publish in an archive, so you can extend the work later.

Structure of a four-page paper

A rule of thumb, not a requirement.

Abstract0.1 p
Introduction0.5 p
Related work0.5 p
Method1 p
Results1 p
Discussion0.75 p
Future0.25 p
Page 04 pages, excluding references

No data? Replace Method and Results with a short argument and a proposed study design.

What reviewers look for

  • A clear, narrow question tied to prior work
  • A method that can answer it, repeatable
  • Honest limits
  • Care for participants
  • Writing a neighboring field can follow
