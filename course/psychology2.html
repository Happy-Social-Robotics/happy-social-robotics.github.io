The psychology of meeting a robot

2. Anthropomorphism

  • Easy human knowledgeWe reach for what we know best: people.
  • Wish to predictA mind makes behavior easier to guess.
  • Wish for connectionLonely people see more humanity.
ClaimPeople vary in how much human they see in nonhuman agents. Three things explain it.
Evidence

Loneliness predicts more anthropomorphism.

Epley et al. 2007
Implication

Participant state matters, not only robot design. Measure or control loneliness, goals and prior knowledge.

Open question

Do the three factors hold over weeks, or wear off with familiarity?
