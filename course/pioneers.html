The pioneers

MM

Masahiro Mori

Roboticist · Japan · uncanny valley, 1970

Mori worked on prosthetic hands. Some looked real but felt cold and strange to touch. In a 1970 essay he proposed that liking rises as a robot looks more human, then drops sharply just before it looks fully human. The dip got its name: the uncanny valley.
