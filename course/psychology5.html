The psychology of meeting a robot

5. Trust

  • Robot performance
  • Appearance
  • Who the user is

How strongly each is linked to trust. Schematic, not data.

ClaimTrust follows what the robot does more than how it looks or who the user is.
Evidence

A review of 29 studies. Reliability had the largest link to trust, appearance a weak one. The evidence base was small.

Hancock et al. 2011
Implication

Check the robot works reliably before testing personality or appearance. A failure can swamp your effect.

Open question

How is trust repaired after an error, and does it differ by role?
