The psychology of meeting a robot

3. The uncanny valley

Schematic after Mori (1970). Placement is approximate, not data.

ClaimAffinity rises with human likeness, drops sharply near human, then recovers at full human likeness.
Evidence

Proposed by Mori in a 1970 essay; translated into English in 2012. He called it a hypothesis, said movement amplifies it, and left the cause open.

Mori 2012
Implication

Cite it as a hypothesis, not a law. If your robot is near-human, test for the dip instead of assuming it.

Open question

What is the mechanism, and does it hold for movement, voice and behavior, not just faces?
