The same loop, in Tega

A robot senses, interprets, decides, expresses, and learns. Then it repeats.

ExampleTega tells a story to a preschooler.

  1. SenseCamera and microphone catch the child’s face and voice
  2. InterpretIs the child leaning in, or looking away?
  3. DecideBored: switch to a more playful prompt
  4. ExpressIts face brightens and it asks a question
  5. LearnRemembers what kept this child engaged
