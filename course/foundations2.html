What makes a robot social?

Five questions to place any robot

  • Who interacts with it?
  • In what setting, and how long?
  • What must it understand about people?
  • What does it express, through which channel?
  • What changes for the person afterward?

Try them on Jackrabbot, Kismet, Tega and PARO. Your answers will differ on almost every line.

A simple loop

A teaching simplification, not Fong et al.’s taxonomy. Ask where a study steps in.

  1. SenseCameras, microphones, touch
  2. InterpretFaces, speech, who is where
  3. DecidePick a response given goals and norms
  4. ExpressGaze, face, voice, movement
  5. LearnUpdate a model of this person
repeats
  • Kismet: Express
  • Jackrabbot: Sense and Decide
  • Tega: adds Learn
