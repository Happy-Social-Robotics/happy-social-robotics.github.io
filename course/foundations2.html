What makes a robot social?

Two traditions called “social”

Collective

Many simple robots coordinate, inspired by social insects.

MembersAnonymous: any one can stand in for another

Individual (this course)

Partners recognize each other, form relationships and follow shared norms.

MembersIndividuals with histories, who communicate and learn from each other

Five questions to place any robot

  • Who interacts with it?
  • In what setting, and how long?
  • What must it understand about people?
  • What does it express, through which channel?
  • What changes for the person afterward?

Try them on Jackrabbot, Kismet, Tega and PARO. Your answers will differ on almost every line.
