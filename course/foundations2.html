A simple loop

A teaching simplification, not Fong et al.’s taxonomy. Ask where a study steps in.

  1. SenseCameras, microphones, touch
  2. InterpretFaces, speech, who is where
  3. DecidePick a response given goals and norms
  4. ExpressGaze, face, voice, movement
  5. LearnUpdate a model of this person
repeats
  • Kismet: Express
  • Jackrabbot: Sense and Decide
  • Tega: adds Learn

Why a body?

ClaimPhysical presence can improve interaction, but it has to earn its cost.
Evidence

Belpaeme et al. credit much of robot tutors’ success to physical presence, and note virtual agents can offer some of it without the expense, upkeep and installation.

Belpaeme et al. 2018
Implication

Say what the body adds: touch, shared space, gaze toward objects. If the answer is “nothing”, test a screen agent instead.

Open question

Which effects come from embodiment and which from novelty? Long-term studies are still rare.
