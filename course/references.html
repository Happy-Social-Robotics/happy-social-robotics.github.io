Glossary

AoE (Anywhere on Earth)
A deadline time zone, UTC-12. A deadline at 23:59 AoE is the last moment of that date anywhere in the world.
Anthropomorphism
Attributing human form, characteristics or behavior to nonhuman things.
Archival and non-archival
Archival papers are published in permanent proceedings and usually count as prior publication. Non-archival ones are shown at the event without that status.
Caricatured robot
A robot with exaggerated or simplified creature-like features that signal social ability.
Companion proceedings
A volume of short or supplementary contributions published alongside the main proceedings.
Double-blind review
Reviewers do not know the authors, and authors do not know the reviewers.
Effect size
The size of a difference or relationship, such as Cohen's d or a correlation r, independent of sample size.
HRI
Human-robot interaction: the field, and the ACM/IEEE conference of that name.
LBR (Late Breaking Report)
A short paper for early-stage results, presented as a poster.
Power analysis
A calculation of how many participants are needed to detect an effect of a given size.
sigconf
The ACM double-column template used for HRI submissions.
Wizard of Oz
A technique in which a hidden person controls part of what the robot seems to do on its own.
Zoomorphic
Animal-like in form.
