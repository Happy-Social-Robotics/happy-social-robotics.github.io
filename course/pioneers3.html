The pioneers

TS

Takanori Shibata

Japan · PARO, the seal robot

Shibata began designing PARO in the 1990s. He chose a baby seal because few people have strong memories of one, so there is no “real” seal to compare it with. PARO now appears in care settings, where people with dementia sometimes respond to it when little else works.

PARO therapeutic robot

A hospice program uses PARO with people who have dementia.
