5

The psychology of meeting a robot

6. People differ

ClaimCulture, prior experience, personality and age all shape how a robot is perceived.
Evidence

Cultural background, experience with robots and personality influence perception. Validated translations of questionnaires are needed. RO-MAN 2027 has a cross-cultural theme.

Bartneck et al. 2009
Implication

Report your sample: age, culture, experience with robots. Do not generalize beyond it.

Open question

Most samples are small and from a few countries. Replication elsewhere is a realistic beginner project.

Read further afieldLabs studying how people respond to avatars and virtual humans ask similar questions. Stanford’s Virtual Human Interaction Lab publishes openly, and findings often transfer to robots.
