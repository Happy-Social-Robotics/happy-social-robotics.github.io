Unit 2 of 8

Quiz: what did you take from the videos?

Twelve questions, one per idea from Unit 1. Choose an answer to see whether it is right and why. Nothing is saved, and you can try again as often as you like.

If you are stuck on a question, go back to the video it came from. The questions follow the order of the videos.

What to do with your score

  • 9 to 12. Continue to Unit 3.
  • 5 to 8. Rewatch the videos where you missed questions, then continue. Units 3 to 7 revisit every idea.
  • 0 to 4. Rewatch Videos 1, 2 and 4 first. They carry the main ideas.
